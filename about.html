Engineering Verification

Our 4-Stage Digital Telemetry Protocol

Every vehicle listing published on MOTORVAULT undergo our multi-point electronic diagnostic audit, ensuring buyers receive un-tampered mechanical reports.

01.

OBD-II ECU Scan

Direct CAN-bus electronic scan detecting stored fault codes, mileage rollback signatures, and airbag system logs.

02.

Paint Depth Audit

Ultrasonic body gauge inspection verifying factory paint thickness and identifying undisclosed accident body repairs.

03.

Chassis Laser Align

Structural subframe geometry verification ensuring zero unibody twisting or suspension mounting fatigue.

04.

Digital Passport

Immutable cryptographic report generated for each verified vehicle, viewable by buyers via QR scan.
